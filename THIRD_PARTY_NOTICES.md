# Third Party Notices

## Multiness

skyTox includes a NES/Famicom emulator module based on Multiness components.

Project: https://github.com/kakashidinho/Multiness_public

The in-app games screen also links to the Multiness project and thanks its
developers for the emulator and multiplayer groundwork.

No `.nes` ROM files are distributed with skyTox. Users place their own `.nes`
files in the `skyTox games` folder and are responsible for the legality of the
files they use.
